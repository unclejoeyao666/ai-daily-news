据彭博社查阅的文件，两家中国公司在上海长三角项目的数据中心安装了英伟达 Blackwell 半导体，其中包括 GB300 NVL72 机架。文件中的序列号被提交给中国监管机构。上海朔曜以这批硬件作抵押，向上海创新银行申请9600万元人民币（约1400万美元）贷款。该数据中心与 Alice Huang 有关联；她是围绕这家美国芯片制造商争议的核心人物。

## 对 AI 行业的影响

影响判断：这则报道把高端AI芯片的实际部署、设备融资和跨境管制风险联系起来。若相关设备受到美国出口限制，供应链溯源与合规审查可能加强，芯片融资和数据中心投资也会承担更高的审查风险；现有材料描述的是文件所示安装及抵押情况，并未给出监管机构的认定。

> A pair of Chinese firms installed Nvidia Corp.’s powerful Blackwell semiconductors in a Shanghai data center linked to Alice Huang, the woman at the heart of a controversy surrounding the US chipmaker. The gear installed at the Yangtze River Delta Project included a GB300 NVL72 rack, among Nvidia’s most powerful products, according to a serial number specified in documents submitted to Chinese regulators in November. Shanghai Shuoyao offered the hardware as collateral to obtain a 96 million yuan ($14 million) loan from Shanghai Innovation Bank, according to filings from the lender that were reviewed by Bloomberg News. Bloomberg's Tom Keene and Paul Sweeney get details from Olivia Levieux, Markets Strategist with Bloomberg News (Source: Bloomberg)

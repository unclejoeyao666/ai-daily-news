据 Bloomberg Technology，Allie Miller 介绍，前沿公司会在财务、法务、市场和销售等部门以较 modest 的订阅级支出提升基础能力，同时设立规模更小、预算更高、拥有更大实验自由度的前沿团队。她还讨论了 Anthropic 和 OpenAI 的相关话题。

## 对 AI 行业的影响

这种分层投资机制意味着企业不会把全部预算押在前沿实验上，而是先用普及型工具覆盖多数部门，再集中资源探索高风险高回报方向；AI 供应商也需同时服务这两类预算。

> Allie Miller, CEO of Open Machine, shared insights on how top technology companies are managing their AI investments to balance cost and return. She explained that frontier companies typically divide their AI efforts into two groups: one focused on raising the baseline capabilities across departments like finance, legal, marketing, and sales with modest subscription-level spending, and a second, smaller 'frontier' team that operates with significantly higher budgets and greater experimentation freedom. She speaks with Romaine Bostick on "The Close." (Source: Bloomberg)

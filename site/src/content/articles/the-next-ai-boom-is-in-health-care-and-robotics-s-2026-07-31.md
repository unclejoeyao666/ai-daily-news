Recent disclosures from Anthropic and OpenAI have shifted the AI conversation from model capabilities to real-world deployment and security. Deena Shakir says the next wave of AI innovation will be built on trust, partnerships and vertical applications like health care and robotics, not just more powerful foundation models.

## 对 AI 行业的影响

这反映出资本对 AI 叙事的重心正在从“更大模型”转向“可落地行业场景”。对创业公司来说，最有价值的不是再讲一遍通用能力，而是拿出能过临床、工厂和责任链条验证的垂直方案。
